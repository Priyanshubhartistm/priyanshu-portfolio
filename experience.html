Where I've worked

A timeline of roles and the skills I've picked up along the way — currently focused on Bitcoin/Nostr backend engineering.

Jul 2026 — Present

Software Engineer · nostream

Internship · Ottawa, Ontario, Canada · Remote

Working on Nostream, a production-ready Nostr relay written in TypeScript (800+ GitHub stars) implementing more than a dozen NIPs. Fixed a crash in the NIP-11 relay-info response when payment settings are absent, an un-awaited Redis EXISTS call that could return stale cache hits, and a bug that let already-rate-limited clients rack up extra limit windows. De-duplicated events returned by generic tag-filter subscriptions, corrected event-expiration edge cases (rejecting timestamp 0, accepting far-future values), and improved the error messages returned on replaceable-event rejections — plus two documentation fixes in CONFIGURATION.md.

Aug 2026 — Present

Dev Fellow · Bitshala

Fellowship · United States · Remote

Fellow at Bitshala, a Bitcoin developer-education community (India / Global South) that places fellows on real Bitcoin open-source projects under mentorship. Continuing backend work on Nostream — most recently shipping a fix to cleanly abort in-flight subscription streams on client disconnect, and building out DVM (Data Vending Machine) worker-registry settings and process topology. Focus areas: Bitcoin and back-end web development.

Feb 2026 — Aug 2026 · 7 mos

Developer · Summer of Bitcoin

Internship · San Francisco, California, United States · Remote

Contributed to Nostream, a production-grade Nostr Relay, as part of the Summer of Bitcoin 2026 cohort — a global internship that matches university students with mentors to contribute to real Bitcoin open-source projects. Implemented integration test coverage for multiple Nostr Improvement Proposals, including NIP-40 (event expiration), NIP-22 (created_at limits), and NIP-04 (encrypted direct messages).
